01 Introduction & Overview

Welcome to LogIt ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by BuildParrot. LogIt is a client tracking, session logging, and management application tailored for practitioners, consultants, coaches, and professionals who demand total confidentiality and control over their client data.

We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Our core philosophy is Local-First and Zero-Knowledge. We do not operate proprietary tracking servers, we do not monetize your data, and we do not monitor your clients or notes.

💡
Key Takeaway: Your records, notes, and profile details are encrypted and stored directly on your physical Android device. Unless you explicitly choose to back up to your own private Google Drive storage, data never leaves your hardware.

02 Information We Handle

Because LogIt runs locally on your device, the information you enter is handled exclusively by your local installation:

A. Client Profiles, Session Information & Media

  • Client Demographics: First name, middle name, last name, phone number, email address, physical address, and date of birth.
  • Client Profile Photos & Vault Media: Profile photos and encrypted client attachments saved locally in client_vault/.
  • Session Records & Notes: Timestamps, session notes, progress tracking, exercise logs, status tags, and associated metadata.
  • Session Photos & Exercise Videos: Photos and video clips captured or attached during workout sessions saved in workout_media/.
  • Emergency Contacts: Emergency contact full name, telephone number, and relation.
  • User Preferences: App lock preferences, biometric toggles, and UI theme options.

B. Technical Data (Non-Identifiable)

LogIt does not collect device telemetry, IP logs, or advertisement identifiers (AAID). Android system crash reports may only be collected if you have globally opted in to share diagnostics via Google Play Services in your Android OS settings.

03 Device Permissions & Usage Details

LogIt adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege. We request only the Android system permissions strictly necessary to deliver core features and user security. Below is a comprehensive breakdown of every permission declared and used by LogIt, including its purpose, trigger mechanism, and data privacy safeguards:

No Address Book Scraping: LogIt never reads, scans, harvests, or uploads your address book. Imported details are immediately encrypted using SQLCipher AES-256 and stored purely in your local database.

Detailed Permission Safeguards & Disclosures

1. Prominent Disclosure on Contacts Access (Google Play Compliant)

In full compliance with Google Play's Prominent Disclosure and Consent policy, LogIt presents an explicit, dedicated disclosure modal inside the app before triggering the Android system contact permission prompt.

  • Clear Explanation: The disclosure explicitly informs you that contact permission is used solely to prefill client details from a single selected contact.
  • User Control: You can choose "Cancel" to decline permission at any time and manually enter client details instead. The app remains 100% functional even if contact permission is denied.

2. Background Tasks & Service Execution

LogIt uses Android WorkManager for automated database backups. Scheduled tasks run periodically in the background only when configured by the user. LogIt does not use invasive foreground service notifications or permanent background processes, and has explicitly removed FOREGROUND_SERVICE permissions.

3. How to Inspect & Revoke Permissions

You maintain complete control over all system permissions granted to LogIt. You can review, grant, or revoke permissions at any time through your device settings:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps (or App Management) > LogIt.
  3. Tap Permissions to view or modify access (e.g., set Contacts to Allow or Don't Allow).
ℹ️
Google Play Policy Guarantee: LogIt does not request any hidden, unused, or dangerous permissions. All permissions declared in the AndroidManifest.xml serve explicit, user-facing core app functions.

04 Google Drive API & Limited Use Disclosure

LogIt provides an optional, encrypted cloud backup and restore capability utilizing Google Drive. If you choose to enable this feature, please review our compliance with Google API policies:

Application Data Folder Scope Only

LogIt requests the restricted Google Drive scope:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (DriveScopes.DRIVE_APPDATA).

  • No Access to Personal Files: The App can only read and write files within its own isolated, hidden Application Data folder in your Google Drive.
  • Complete Isolation: LogIt cannot view, list, read, modify, or delete any of your personal documents, photos, spreadsheets, or other folders stored in your Google Drive.

Comprehensive Encrypted Backups (Database & Media)

When cloud backup is performed, LogIt automatically packages your encrypted SQLCipher database snapshot together with all stored client profile photos, session images, and exercise video clips into a unified, AES-256-GCM encrypted ZIP archive before syncing to Google Drive. This guarantees 100% complete restoration of all records and media files when restoring a backup.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

LogIt's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  1. We use Google Drive access solely to provide backup and synchronization capabilities requested by the user.
  2. We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties.
  3. We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or profiling.
  4. Human beings do not read your Google user data or backup files.

05 Data Security & Architecture

We deploy defense-in-depth security best practices to protect your information:

  • SQLCipher AES-256 Encryption: Your Room database is encrypted at rest on your physical flash storage using SQLCipher 256-bit encryption.
  • Android Keystore Integration: Encryption keys are bound to your device hardware via Android Keystore and Jetpack Security (EncryptedSharedPreferences).
  • Biometric Lockouts: Optional biometric and PIN authentication ensures that unauthorized individuals cannot open LogIt even if your device is unlocked.
  • No Developer Backdoors: We have no administrative accounts, remote login portals, or backdoor keys. You are the sole custodian of your database.

06 Third-Party Services & Libraries

LogIt does not include any advertising networks, behavioral analytics platforms, or social network tracking SDKs.

The only third-party service provider involved is:

  • Google LLC (Google Play Services / Google Drive API): Utilized solely when you choose to connect Google Drive for backing up or restoring your application database. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

07 Data Retention & Complete Deletion

Because you retain 100% ownership and custody of your data, you can delete your data at any time without submitting a request:

How to Delete Your Data:

  • Delete Within App: You can edit or permanently delete individual client cards, session logs, and notes within LogIt.
  • Clear All Local Data: Open your device Settings > Apps > LogIt > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data. This instantly erases all local encrypted databases and keys.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling LogIt removes all application directories and local sandbox files from your phone.
  • Remove Cloud Backups: You can delete backups directly within the LogIt backup screen, or navigate to Google Drive on the web > Settings > Manage Apps > LogIt > Options > Delete hidden app data.

08 Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA Compliant)

Regardless of your geographic location (including the European Economic Area under GDPR and California under CCPA/CPRA), LogIt naturally provides full self-service fulfillment of your statutory privacy rights:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can view all records directly in the app or export/backup your database file at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit any client record, telephone number, or log instantly inside the application.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Erase records locally or wipe app data as detailed in Section 07.
  • No Sale or Sharing of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or share personal data under CCPA/CPRA.
